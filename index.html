A note on accuracy

Most words are shown using a fingerspelling sequence. A few common words have simple illustrative icons. Real sign languages have their own grammar, regional variation, hand movement, orientation, and facial expressions.

These drawings are schematic learning visuals, not authoritative ASL instruction.
