Train on your own hand

Pick a letter, make the shape, press Record, and hold it for 3 seconds while moving your hand a little. Repeat for each letter. With two or more letters trained, the page uses the samples instead of the built-in rules. Samples you record are added to any that ship with the project.

A custom word is one held hand shape. Add a photo named after it (for example hello.jpg) to the alphabet folder to show it in text → sign.

Open hand with thumb out adds a space. For J and Z, draw the letter in the air.

Sign language word guide

Search common ASL glosses and load a word into the translator. Alphabet photos are used for fingerspelling unless a matching custom sign has been trained and added as an image.

A glossary is not a trained sign recogniser. Real ASL uses handshape, orientation, location, movement, two-hand relationships, facial expression and grammar. This guide does not claim to show authoritative sign videos.
